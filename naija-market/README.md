# Naija Market

A Nigerian and pan-African maker goods storefront concept. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview. Search, category filtering, sorting, saved items, cart quantities, and the demo checkout are interactive. Cart and saved items persist in local storage.

## Photography

Photography is served from Unsplash and Pexels and requires an internet connection. The images were selected from documentary photos of a Lagos fabric market, African basket weaving, pottery making in Nigeria, and art in Lekki. Unsplash license: https://unsplash.com/license. Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/.

Image sources include Unsplash photographers Ben Iwara, Dwayne Joe, Dee Dee, Blessfield John, Lisa Marie Theck, Omar, and Tanya Barrow, and the Pexels Nigeria pottery photo by Darkshade Photos.

## Commerce setup

This portfolio concept does not process payments or submit real orders. Replace the sample products and contact information, and connect a commerce backend and payment provider before launch.
